# Third-party acknowledgments

## End-to-End Test-Time Training

The native language experiments build on **End-to-End Test-Time Training for
Long Context**. We thank its authors for releasing the research and the
[official JAX implementation](https://github.com/test-time-training/e2e).
This repository contains a PyTorch runtime and additional experimental and
analysis code for studying persistent self-generated feedback.

Consult the upstream repository for its license and citation. Our MIT license
does not replace licenses attached to third-party code, datasets, or weights.
